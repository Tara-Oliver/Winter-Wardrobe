BUILDING YOUR seasonal wardrobe

For winter, layering is key! So your wardrobe basics should continue to play the role of your connecting items and assist with building your looks. As seasons change and we shift from warm weather to cooler temperatures, it’s time to begin evaluating our wardrobe and focusing on those pieces that will carry you through the coming season and many seasons beyond.

ADDING IN trends

When building your wardrobe, don’t place too much emphasis on the latest trends. Keep in mind that the purpose of building a wardrobe is that it lasts through the seasons, year after year. The key here is timeless and classic pieces that will never go out of style. This way, you never have to ask if the item is still “on trend” because the answer is, and always will be, yes. Trends may or may not have staying power, so why spend time focusing on building with trends or trendy items that may not last? This isn’t to say you can’t incorporate trends; they should simply complement those timeless and classic pieces

SPLURGE vs. SAVE

I always like to suggest adding these recommended items in different styles, colors, shapes, and/or prints. By doing that, you create options. You’re faced with a choice: Invest $200 in a sweater or spend just $20 on a sweater. Beside the price points and the quality being different, why can’t the $20 sweater last just as long as the $200 sweater? It can and it will … if you properly care for your garments. However, I highly recommend investing in quality outerwear, shoes, and bags. Bags may be case by case since you have quality bags and then those that are on trend or not practical for everyday use. Outerwear and shoes can be pricier but definitely worth the splurge and more cost-effective than rotating different pairs or styles in and out each season. You do not need a new coat or shoe collection each year. On the contrary, you only need to update a few pieces, by choice. Save on the pieces you can easily update or replace that lack a hefty price tag and invest in those timeless and classic pieces
